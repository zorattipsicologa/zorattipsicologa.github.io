I am **Dr. Giulia Maria Zoratti, psychologist and cognitive-behavioral psychotherapist**.
I practice in Bolzano, Trento, and online.
I offer professional support for a wide range of difficulties, including:

- **anxiety**
- **stress**
- **phobias**
- **panic attacks**
- **depression**
- **hypochondria**
- **autism**
- **learning disorders**, helping students find a faster and more effective study method.

My core strengths are empathy, professionalism, warmth, and a non-judgmental attitude towards different life paths and sensitivities.

Over the years I have had the opportunity to collaborate with various organizations:

- Psychologist at GAM Medical (2025-present)
- Psychologist at the Observation, Diagnosis and Training Laboratory (ODF), Università di Trento (2023-2024)
- Psychologist at Azienda Provinciale per i Servizi Sanitari di Trento (2022, 2025)
- Psychologist at Azienda Sanitaria Giuliano Isontina, Child and Adolescent Health Unit (2021)
- Psychologist at Associazione per i Bambini Chirurgici del Burlo ONLUS (2020)
- I also have several years of experience supporting study skills, working with both university students and students with learning disorders.

I work with schools and associations to improve academic performance for students with Specific Learning Disorders (SLD), focusing not only on academic goals but also on motivation and self-esteem, addressing the needs of the whole person.
